What the new C_TS452_2601 exam looks like

SAP has moved its certification exams to a performance-based format. For Sourcing & Procurement (MM), that means C_TS452_2601: hands-on tasks executed inside a real SAP S/4HANA system, not multiple-choice questions.

The exam is open-book — SAP Help Portal, SAP Learning, SAP Community, and Joule for Consultants are available during the exam. It is non-proctored. Scoring is based on the system outputs of your completed tasks, reviewed automatically or via evaluation scripts.

Sources: SAP Learning · Certification practical exam FAQ · SAP Learning · Exam essentials · SAP Community · Practical exams roll-out 2026.

Specifics of the C_TS452_2601 exam (duration, exact task count, topic weights) are kept current on the official SAP certification page. The course material is aligned to that page, not to outdated third-party syllabi.

Three days. End-to-end. With a real instructor.

Sessions are live on Teams or Zoom. You configure and execute alongside the instructor in a real SAP system. Recordings are available afterwards for review.

Curriculum is aligned with the SAP Learning Hub modules behind the official certification path: S4500 (Procurement Processes), S4510 (Inventory Management), S4515 (Invoice Verification), S4520 (Purchasing), S45250 (Consumption-Based Planning), and S4550 (Cross-Functional Customizing) — roughly 54 hours of official exercise material compressed into three focused days.

Day 1

Procurement foundation & master data

  • End-to-end procure-to-pay: purchase order, goods receipt, invoice — Fiori apps and back-end transactions side by side
  • Stock material vs. consumable material — what changes in postings and FI impact
  • Master data deep-dive: Business Partner for suppliers, material master extension, purchasing info records, conditions
  • Enterprise structure: purchasing organization, plant, valuation classes, account assignment categories

Aligned with SAP S4500 + S4520 (Master Data) + S4550 (Valuation & Account Assignment)

Day 2

Advanced purchasing, sources of supply & MRP

  • Contracts, scheduling agreements, source lists, quota arrangements — when to use which
  • Source determination: automatic vs. manual, behaviour inside the MRP run
  • Purchase requisition and purchase order release procedures, flexible workflows
  • Specific procurement: subcontracting, blanket POs, invoicing plans, consignment, pipeline
  • Consumption-based planning, reorder point, MRP areas, forecasting basics

Aligned with SAP S4520 (Purchasing) + S45250 (Consumption-Based Planning & Forecasting)

Day 3

Inventory, invoice verification & exam strategy

  • Goods receipts in all variants: with/without reference, blocked stock, returns, free-of-charge, production order
  • Goods issues, stock transfers (storage location and plant), reservations
  • Physical inventory, cycle counting, ABC analysis, situation handling
  • Invoice entry, variances and blocks, GR/IR account maintenance, evaluated receipt settlement
  • Closing: how to read a system-based task quickly, when to consult SAP Help vs. just configure, time-management across the exam

Aligned with SAP S4510 (Inventory Management) + S4515 (Invoice Verification) + exam-strategy closing block

Day-by-day order may shift slightly per cohort based on group level. Topics may be deepened or compressed where the cohort needs more time — small-group format makes that possible.
