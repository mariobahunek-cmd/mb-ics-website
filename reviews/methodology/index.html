How participant reviews work

English

Participant reviews on Mario Bahunek’s website are managed by the operator, MB International Contracting Service LLC. This is not an independent rating platform. This feedback supports training partners and corporate clients; it is not an exam or learning portal.

Submission and verification

No participant account is needed. Give an overall rating from 1 to 5, an optional original comment, a training area and an optional course month. Choose anonymity or a first name/initials. Your email stays private. Publication requires separate voluntary consent (notice version 2026-10-09), email confirmation and moderation. Grades and certificates are unaffected; positive reviews receive no reward. Email confirmation verifies an email address only, not course attendance. We do not claim verified participation without documented evidence.

Moderation, including criticism

All ratings follow the same rules. We reject only spam, duplicates, third-party personal data, unlawful content or content unrelated to training. Criticism is not a reason to reject a review. We do not rewrite original feedback for marketing. Necessary personal-data redactions are documented; substantial changes require author contact. Reviews move through pending email, pending moderation, published, rejected or withdrawn. Administrative changes are audited.

Historical evidence and limitations

Two evidence groups, not three competing collections: (1) anonymous historical course evaluations from COMCAVE teaching assignments, and (2) personal training reviews and recommendations, collected with continuity across three channels — earlier LinkedIn recommendations, later ProvenExpert, and the direct submission system on this website (own 1–5 scale, 5 best) that replaces the paid service. The original evaluation table is published as an unaltered image: view/download the original evaluation. COMCAVE 1.07 measures preparation, not overall instructor quality. Four questions use the 1–6 school grade scale (1 best): preparation 1.07 (n=107), structure 1.10 (n=107), subject expertise 1.08 (n=107), didactics 1.14 (n=108). Two questions are yes/no: active involvement 106 yes / 0 no (100%), enough time to consolidate 104 yes / 2 no (98.1%); their 1.00 and 1.02 figures are binary means, not school grades. That is 106–108 answers per question, not 108 unique people; the original states no evaluation period. Approx. 300 participants and >98% exam pass rate are separate operator claims with no locally evidenced exam denominator. LinkedIn quotations are selected excerpts/translations, not a complete or random sample. No per-recommendation original URLs/dates are evidenced locally; Mario’s linked profile is not an exact recommendation link. Email/chat voices are labeled separately. Do not infer attendance verification, independent endorsement or reuse rights from their presence. ProvenExpert figures and promotional awards are undated source claims within the recommendation group, never part of a pooled or website average.

Counting and sources

The homepage shows the count and at most the three most recent cards of published native website reviews; it displays no average across sources. If the review service itself reports a mean or a 1–5 distribution, that covers exactly the published native website reviews — never LinkedIn, ProvenExpert or historical items — and is never pooled. Rounding happens only for display. With no published reviews the review block stays hidden, so no count or average appears. The load-more control paginates the recommendations wall of authorized historical and LinkedIn items. There is no sort by lowest rating. We preserve original language; we do not provide unlabelled translations. A review count is not a count of unique people.

LinkedIn recommendations, evaluation grades, pass rates and participant statistics remain separate. Authorized historical reviews are not kept in a separate archive: they are integrated into the homepage recommendations wall with load-more, each labelled with its source, original rating, scale and original date. No decimal score is silently rounded to whole stars. Website reviews do not guarantee Google stars or review rich results; this version contains no AggregateRating markup.

Optional LinkedIn handoff

After submission every author, whatever the rating, may copy only their own original comment and open Mario’s profile. A recommendation requires a first-degree connection. Connect first if necessary, then find More → Recommend or its current equivalent. Mario decides whether to display it. There is no automatic posting, login, prefill, star transfer or requirement to use LinkedIn. Rating-only reviews generate no text. Clipboard failure offers manual copying and does not affect your saved website review.

Privacy, correction and withdrawal

Contact mario@mb-ics.com for access, correction or deletion, or use the private withdrawal link in your verification email. Withdrawing removes the review from public results and statistics and erases the associated private email and comment. Unconfirmed submissions are deleted after 14 days, rejected submissions after 30 days; short-lived abuse keys expire within one day. Published reviews remain while consent and purpose continue, subject to review and withdrawal. Restricted audits contain action metadata, not email or review text. Backups require restricted access, a maximum 30-day rotation and reapplication of withdrawals before restoration is made public. See the review privacy notice.

Zählung und Quellen

Die Startseite zeigt die Anzahl und höchstens die drei neuesten Karten veröffentlichter nativer Website-Bewertungen; sie zeigt keinen quellenübergreifenden Durchschnitt. Meldet der Bewertungsdienst selbst einen Mittelwert oder eine Sterneverteilung, bezieht sich das ausschließlich auf veröffentlichte native Website-Bewertungen — nie auf LinkedIn, ProvenExpert oder historische Einträge — und wird nie gepoolt. Rundung erfolgt erst bei der Anzeige. Ohne Veröffentlichungen bleibt der Bewertungsbereich ausgeblendet, also keine Anzahl und kein Durchschnitt. »Mehr laden« blättert die Empfehlungsübersicht aus autorisierten Altbewertungen und LinkedIn-Empfehlungen. Eine Sortierung nach niedrigster Bewertung gibt es nicht. Die Originalsprache bleibt erhalten; keine unmarkierten Übersetzungen. Bewertungsanzahlen entsprechen nicht eindeutig bewertenden Personen.

LinkedIn-Empfehlungen, Evaluationsnoten, Prüfungsquoten und Teilnehmerzahlen bleiben getrennt. Autorisierte Altbewertungen liegen nicht in einem separaten Archiv: sie sind mit »Mehr laden« in die Empfehlungsübersicht der Startseite integriert, jeweils mit Quellenangabe, Originalwertung, Skala und Originaldatum. Dezimalwerte werden nicht still auf ganze Sterne gerundet. Kein Versprechen von Google-Sternen oder Review-Rich-Results; diese Version enthält kein AggregateRating-Markup.

Bei Bedarf zuerst vernetzen, dann Mehr → Empfehlen oder die aktuelle Entsprechung wählen. Mario entscheidet über die Anzeige. Kein automatisches Posten, Login, Vorbefüllen, Übertragen von Sternen oder Nutzungszwang. Aus reinen Sternebewertungen wird kein Text erzeugt. Bei Zwischenablagefehlern ist manuelles Kopieren möglich; die gespeicherte Website-Bewertung bleibt erhalten.

Datenschutz, Korrektur und Widerruf

Kurzlebige Missbrauchsschlüssel verfallen innerhalb eines Tages. Veröffentlichte Bewertungen bleiben bei fortbestehendem Zweck und Einwilligung, unter Vorbehalt von Prüfung und Widerruf.
