Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

MB International Contracting Service LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates the websites mb-ics.com and berater.mb-ics.com. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use either website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you use our contact form:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Company name (optional)
  • Training interest / message content

This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry about our SAP training services.

For the SAP MM consultant-trainer private beta application form hosted at berater.mb-ics.com, we collect the information that you voluntarily submit:

  • Full name and business email address
  • Company and role
  • SAP MM experience band
  • Your company's frequency of SAP training
  • The type of legitimate SAP system access owned by you or your company
  • Feedback commitment and preferred language
  • Motivation (optional)
  • Your required acknowledgement of the privacy information for the application

2. Automatic Data Collection

Our website uses the browser's localStorage feature and — subject to your consent — first/third-party cookies set by analytics and advertising services. We store the following essential items locally without analytics or marketing consent:

  • Your language preference (German, English, or Spanish)
  • Your consent choice (whether you accepted analytics and marketing cookies)

If you consent to analytics and marketing storage, click-attribution identifiers from advertising platforms (gclid, gbraid, wbraid) and campaign parameters (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign) may be stored in localStorage for up to 90 days to associate later inquiries with the campaign that brought you here. No direct personal identifier is stored alongside these values.

localStorage is not a cookie — it is a browser-based storage mechanism that does not expire automatically and is not transmitted with HTTP requests.

3. Consent Mechanism (Google Consent Mode v2)

When you first visit our website, a consent banner is shown. Until you make a choice, our analytics service is loaded in a "consent-denied" mode (no personalized cookies, no identifiers transmitted). You can choose between:

  • "Accept all" – Allows analytics (Google Analytics 4) to use cookies and identifiers. Also allows embedded YouTube video playback.
  • "Necessary only" – Keeps analytics in consent-denied mode. Google may still receive anonymous cookie-less pings for aggregate modelling; no personal identifiers are sent. YouTube videos remain blocked.

You can change your consent at any time by clicking the "Cookie settings" link in the page footer or by clearing your browser's localStorage for this site (Settings → Site Data), which will cause the consent banner to reappear on your next visit.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For visitors from the European Union and European Economic Area, we process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contact form submissions – Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: Processing is necessary for pre-contractual measures at your request.
  • Crash Course notification-signup form – Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR: Based on your explicit consent via the opt-in checkbox.
  • Private-beta applications – Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: Processing is necessary to take steps at your request before and for beta participation, including receiving, assessing, and replying to applications, sending a receipt, selecting participants, and administering the beta. The privacy acknowledgement is required to submit an application; it is not consent to this core processing. No optional marketing processing occurs as part of the private-beta application.
  • Vercel hosting, security, and global abuse counter – Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: Our legitimate interests are securely and reliably delivering berater.mb-ics.com, preventing abuse, and protecting the application endpoint. The one-hour KV counter is global and contains no applicant email address or IP address.
  • Google Analytics 4 – Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR: Based on your explicit consent via the cookie banner. Click-attribution identifiers (gclid, gbraid, wbraid) and UTM parameters from incoming ad clicks may be stored in localStorage for up to 90 days on the basis of your consent.
  • YouTube video playback – Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR: Based on your explicit consent via our consent banner or the per-video consent prompt.
  • Language preference (localStorage) – Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: Legitimate interest in providing a usable, multilingual website. No personal data is collected or transmitted.

6. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through our contact form exclusively to:

  • Respond to your training inquiries
  • Provide information about our SAP S/4HANA courses
  • Schedule training sessions

We use private-beta application data only to receive and assess applications, send and confirm receipt, reply to applicants, select participants, and administer selected participants in the SAP MM consultant-trainer private beta. Submitting an application does not subscribe you to newsletters or unrelated marketing.

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

7. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which they were collected, typically for the duration of our business communication with you.

MB-ICS deletes its mailbox copy of rejected or non-selected private-beta applications no later than 90 days after cohort selection closes. For selected participants, MB-ICS retains its application copy during the beta and deletes it within 6 months after participation ends, except that specific records may be retained longer where legal obligations require.

Resend separately retains transactional message content, delivery metadata, and operational copies under its service terms and Data Processing Addendum. Its DPA ties general deletion to the customer-account lifecycle and also permits customer deletion instructions; therefore Resend's retention may extend beyond the MB-ICS mailbox periods above. You may ask us at mario@mb-ics.com to send an applicable deletion instruction to Resend.

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU/EEA, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • Right of access (Art. 15) – Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) – Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17) – Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to restrict processing (Art. 18) – Request limitation of data processing.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20) – Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format where the statutory requirements apply.
  • Right to object (Art. 21) – Object to processing based on legitimate interests where the statutory requirements apply.
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)) – Withdraw consent-based processing at any time. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Right to lodge a complaint (Art. 77) – Lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection supervisory authority in the EU/EEA.

Cookie and localStorage consent choices can be changed through the "Cookie settings" link or by clearing localStorage, as described in Section 3. Clearing localStorage does not withdraw a private-beta application.

To exercise any of these rights, including requesting deletion or ending consideration of a private-beta application, contact us at mario@mb-ics.com. A withdrawal or deletion request may end application consideration or participation where the requested data is necessary for those purposes.

If you are accessing our website from the European Union, European Economic Area, or any other region with specific data protection laws, please be aware that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where our company is based.
