Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 9, 2026

MB International Contracting Service LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website mb-ics.com. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you use our contact form:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Company name (optional)
  • Training interest / message content

This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry about our SAP training services.

2. Automatic Data Collection

Our website does not use cookies for tracking or analytics purposes. We use the browser's localStorage feature to store:

  • Your language preference (German, English, or Spanish)
  • Your consent choice (whether you accepted third-party content)

This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party. localStorage is not a cookie — it is a browser-based storage mechanism that does not expire and is not sent with HTTP requests.

3. Consent Mechanism

When you first visit our website, a consent banner is shown. You can choose between:

  • "Accept all" – Allows YouTube video playback (which transmits data to Google).
  • "Necessary only" – Blocks all third-party content. YouTube videos will not load. If you later click a video, you will be asked for consent before any data is transmitted.

You can change your consent at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage for this site (Settings → Site Data), which will cause the consent banner to reappear on your next visit.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services:

  • Fonts – We use the fonts DM Sans and Playfair Display, which are hosted locally on our own server. No connection to external font services (such as Google Fonts) is established when you visit our website. No data is transmitted to third parties for font loading.
  • YouTube (Privacy-Enhanced Mode) – Our video section contains embedded YouTube videos. Videos are embedded using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which reduces tracking. Videos are loaded only when you click to play them and only after you have given consent. YouTube/Google may set cookies when you interact with the video player. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages – Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical log information (IP addresses, browser type) as part of their hosting service. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Formspree – Our contact form submissions are processed through Formspree. When you submit the contact form, your data (name, email, company, message) is transmitted to Formspree's servers. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures). See Formspree's Privacy Policy.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For visitors from the European Union and European Economic Area, we process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contact form submissions – Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: Processing is necessary for pre-contractual measures at your request.
  • YouTube video playback – Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR: Based on your explicit consent via our consent banner or the per-video consent prompt.
  • Language preference (localStorage) – Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: Legitimate interest in providing a usable, multilingual website. No personal data is collected or transmitted.

6. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through our contact form exclusively to:

  • Respond to your training inquiries
  • Provide information about our SAP S/4HANA courses
  • Schedule training sessions

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

7. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which they were collected, typically for the duration of our business communication with you.

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU/EEA, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • Right of access (Art. 15) – Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) – Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17) – Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to restrict processing (Art. 18) – Request limitation of data processing.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20) – Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)) – Withdraw consent at any time (e.g., by clearing localStorage or contacting us). This does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Right to lodge a complaint (Art. 77) – You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in your EU member state.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at mario@mb-ics.com.

9. International Users

Our website is accessible worldwide and our training services are offered internationally. If you are accessing our website from the European Union, European Economic Area, or any other region with specific data protection laws, please be aware that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where our company is based.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for business professionals and are not directed at individuals under the age of 18.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
